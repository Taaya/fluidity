# Project Context

For product strategy, positioning, feature prioritization, and user-flow decisions, read
`PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md` and `PRODUCT_FLOW_REVIEW.md` first and use them as the shared product
context.

Keep a clear distinction between the core product direction, current hypotheses, and validated
behavior. In particular, teacher workflows, reporting, and referrals are hypotheses to test before
they become roadmap commitments.
